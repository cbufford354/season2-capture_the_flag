- I am most proud of the organization of my code. Starting this was very daunting and stumped me for a little bit. I ended up dividing my different creeps into four different groups based upon the body types that they had, close range attackers, long range attackers, healers, and suppliers.
- Then once I had this designation I wrote functions that would individualize for each groups there role that I wanted. This seperation of roles made building the code much easier. Once I had this in place I decided to start with a defesnize strategy having all my creeps stay at my flag and defend.
- Then I pivotted towards activating my tower so that my home defense would favor me when people came rushing in. Then at the tail end I started adding in some offense by creating conditions to when my creeps would be defensive. One condition was that after the 1000th tick all my creeps would go attack no mater what.
- Another was that whenever I had more creeps than my oponent I would go on the offensive ( which I ended up adding a cooldown to). This allowed me to be quite competitive in the in class tournament. My defensive strategy trapped most of my opponents and allowed me to take out creeps in a bunch. Moving forward I wanted to ceep making my creeps more offensive by adding  single targetting by all of my creeps(all my creeps attack the same creep) allowing for more efficient attacking.
- Overall I think that traditional conditional if else statements were the most useful for me because they allowed me to be very responsive to my opponents actions.
